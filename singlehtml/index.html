Multi-cell list-tables¶

sphinx_misc_rkdarst.list_table_multicell adds a list-table-multicell directive, which is list-table with a marker read from the start of a cell: multirow(n) to cover n rows, multicolumn(n) to cover n columns, and multirow(0) or multicolumn(0) in the cells that a span covers. The whole of it is in the docstring of the module.
